REGULATORY GUIDE

Pack responsibly. Ship lawfully.

This is a practical summary, not legal advice. Always check the official source and destination-specific requirements before shipping.

Before booking

Prohibited & dangerous goodsInternational shipment checksAccurate declarationOfficial sources
01

Do not tender prohibited or unsafe items

Explosives, flammables, dangerous goods, narcotics and items prohibited by law must not be sent. Sharp items must be protected; certain batteries may require surface transport.

India Post: prohibited items ↗
02

International shipments need customs checks

Courier imports and exports are subject to Customs rules. Restricted or prohibited goods may need authorisation or may not be exportable at all.

CBIC courier regulations ↗
03

Describe the contents accurately

Use a truthful description and value. For exports, check the current DGFT export policy and ITC(HS) conditions before dispatch.

DGFT export policy notes ↗

Sources: Department of Posts, Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs, and Directorate General of Foreign Trade. Rules can change; OptiGo does not accept prohibited goods.
