GuidelinesCheck what you can safely send

Pack responsibly. Ship lawfully.

Check the item and declaration guidance before you book. This is a practical summary, not legal advice.

Restricted and unsafe items

Do not send explosives, flammables, dangerous goods, narcotics or items prohibited by law. Protect sharp items. Some batteries may need surface transport.

India Post prohibited items ↗

Customs requirements

Courier imports and exports are subject to Customs rules. Restricted goods may require authorisation or may not be exportable.

CBIC courier regulations ↗

Accurate contents and value

Describe the parcel contents and value truthfully. For exports, check the current DGFT policy and ITC(HS) conditions.

DGFT export policy notes ↗

Sources: Department of Posts, CBIC and DGFT. Rules can change. OptiGo does not accept prohibited goods.
